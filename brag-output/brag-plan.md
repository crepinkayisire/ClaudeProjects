# Brag Plan: Kayko Receipts

## What is this app?
Kayko Receipts turns a merchant's plain paper receipt into an itemized digital receipt customers can instantly view and share on WhatsApp — no crumpled paper, no dead-end sale.

## The angle
The project's own pitch is a network-effects play: every receipt Kayko already prints is an untapped growth channel. In June alone, 2,974 unique customers left their phone number with Kayko merchants — and nothing was done with that connection. The video dramatizes the moment a plain receipt becomes a branded, shareable handshake: paper in, WhatsApp tap out.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A big, bold number slams onto the screen: **2,974** — "customers left their number last month." The stat is real, specific, and sets the stakes before the product ever appears.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The "My Receipts" list: real merchant rows (Top Loaf Shop, Eden Coffee, Prime) with color-coded initial badges and a small 🌱 eco badge, arriving one by one.
- Tapping a receipt: the details drawer slides up from the bottom with the itemized purchase (Espresso, Latte, Cappuccino…), subtotal, 10% tax, and bold total — the paper-to-pixel moment.
- The action row at the bottom of the drawer: WhatsApp (green), Download (blue), Print (orange), Share (purple) — the WhatsApp button gets tapped, turning the receipt into a message in one motion.

## Outro / punchline
"Every receipt, a handshake." — Kayko Receipts. Turning paper trails into growth.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: My Receipts list (branded purchase history) → key action: tap a receipt row → result: itemized drawer slides up with tax/total, then the WhatsApp button is tapped to share it.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clean fintech product demo — confident, specific numbers, no jokes, reads like a real feature announcement for a real merchant tool.
- Interpretation: title-case feature labels, clean slide/wipe transitions, 5 scenes, moderate pacing with enough hold time to read the stat and the itemized list.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920 (native to WhatsApp/Instagram Stories, matching the product's own share target)
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f8f9fa` (page), `#fff` (cards, header, drawer)
- Accent: `#6366f1` (indigo — primary CTA/focus); WhatsApp `#22c55e` / `#25d366` for the standout share action
- Text: `#000` primary, `#666` secondary, `#888` tertiary labels
- Display/body font: Inter (weights 400–700)
- Strongest visual element: the receipt list → bottom-drawer transition, plus the colorful merchant-initial avatar badges with the 🌱 eco badge

## Share copy (draft)
Every receipt is now a handshake — Kayko turns paper trails into growth, one WhatsApp tap at a time.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, confident business-pop bed with tasteful UI SFX on taps and reveals
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (~120.19 BPM) — bright and businesslike, not comedic
- Music treatment: starts at 0s under the hook; holds a steady groove through the list and drawer; brief lift under the WhatsApp tap; fades out over the final ~1.5s of the outro card
- Music cue guidance: preset available (120.19 BPM, beat grid ~every 0.5s from 3.02s). Target strong cues near ~3.0s (hook stat lands), ~17.0s (WhatsApp tap), ~18.5s (outro card lands). Receipt-row reveals in Scene 2 may snap to the beat grid for a staggered arrival.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — restrained, professional; no waveform bars or reactive glow
- SFX posture: moderate; soft card-arrival tick per receipt row, gentle whoosh for the drawer sliding up, a crisp UI click on the WhatsApp tap
- Audio-coupled moments: receipt rows arriving one by one (Scene 2), drawer slide-up whoosh (Scene 3), WhatsApp tap click (Scene 4)
- Restraint rule: no comedic stingers, no audio-reactive pulsing — this is a serious merchant feature, not a parody

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook / Stat — 3s
Full-bleed brand-light background (`#f8f9fa`). Big bold number "2,974" slams in and counts up from 0 over ~1s, then settles with the line "customers left their number last month" beneath it in Inter.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confident opening thud that sets the stakes
Audio-coupled idea: the count-up ticks in sync with the early beat grid
Music: bed starts, steady groove
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal: My Receipts — 4s
Recreate the phone-frame "My Receipts" header and list. Three real receipt rows arrive one by one — Top Loaf Shop Ltd. (6,050 RWF), Eden Coffee (5,830 RWF), Prime (13,200 RWF) — each with its color-coded initials badge.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three rows slide/fade in one by one, each with a soft card-arrival tick, snapped to the beat grid
Audio intent: light, rhythmic, builds anticipation
Audio-coupled idea: per-row arrival tick
Music: steady groove continues
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Key action: tap a receipt — 4s
A simulated tap lands on the Eden Coffee row. The details drawer slides up from the bottom with a whoosh: itemized list (Espresso, Latte, Cappuccino, Americano, Mocha), subtotal, "Tax 10%", and a bold total settling in.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap on the row, drawer slides up, line items settle in quickly
Audio intent: satisfying and tactile — the product's "aha" moment
Audio-coupled idea: whoosh on the drawer's slide-up, soft ticks as line items settle
Music: groove continues with a slight lift
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Key action: WhatsApp share — 4s
Camera holds on the drawer's bottom action row (WhatsApp / Download / Print / Share). A simulated tap lands on the green WhatsApp button with a brief press state. Overlay text: "One tap. Straight to WhatsApp."
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap on the WhatsApp button
Audio intent: the payoff — crisp, confident
Audio-coupled idea: UI tap click aligned to the ~17.0s strong beat
Music: strong beat hit
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro / punchline — 5s
Kayko logo/wordmark centered on the brand-light background. Line: "Every receipt, a handshake." Beneath it: "Kayko Receipts." Small tagline: "Turning paper trails into growth."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: warm, confident resolution
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: fades out over the final ~1.5s
Transition mood: soft (end)
